Apple Music to Spotify

Runs in your browser. Your song list goes from this page straight to Spotify and nowhere else. There are no song limits.

1. Connect Spotify

One-time setup. You need Spotify Premium, because Spotify only lets Premium accounts create developer apps.

How to get a Client ID
  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in. On iPhone, use the aA menu in Safari and choose Request Desktop Website if the page looks cut off.
  2. Create app. Any name and description. Under Redirect URIs, add the address below exactly as shown, including the final slash.
  3. Tick Web API, save, then open the app's Settings and copy the Client ID.

2. Add your songs

Choose a file or paste the list.

Accepted formats

One song per line as Title | Artist | Album (album optional). A line starting with # begins a playlist, and the songs under it go in that playlist. Songs above the first # line go to Liked Songs.

# Road trip
Take On Me | a-ha | Hunting High and Low
Africa | Toto

Not in a playlist | Some Artist

Also accepted: CSV or tab-separated files with Name (or Title), Artist, and optionally Album and Playlist columns, text exported from the Music app on a Mac, and JSON.

3. Transfer

Keep this page open with the screen on while it runs. iPhone pauses pages in the background. If it gets interrupted, open the page and press Start again: matches already found are kept.
